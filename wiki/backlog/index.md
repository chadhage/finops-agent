## CD3 scoring and deterministic force rank

**CD3 = relative Cost of Delay / relative Delivery Duration.** This is a relative planning proxy, not a measured economic rate.

- **CoD:** one integer from 1 to 10, representing relative loss-of-value per common delay interval. Analyst estimates consider explicit need, breadth of blocked work, trust/control risk, and stated scope. There is no hidden weighted sum or invented dollar amount.
- **Scale:** 1-3 = limited/deferred option; 4-6 = moderate enablement or uncertain upside; 7-8 = material supported need; 9-10 = broad blocker or basic correctness/control risk. A high score is not a claim of an actual security incident or dated deadline.
- **Duration:** 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, or 13 normalized delivery-duration units. One unit is the smallest complete reviewable slice; larger values represent relatively longer delivery including verification. They are **not days, sprints, or story points**, and no staffing or parallelism is assumed.
- **Confidence:** low for the 58 original and 42 new variant estimates; none is a validated engineering duration or financial delay rate. BL-059 through BL-062 remain unestimated. CD3 estimate confidence is not the agent's statistical qualification.
- **Ordering:** BL-059 through BL-062 remain pinned at ranks 1-4 without CD3 inputs. The explicitly approved scenario band BL-063..BL-104 occupies ranks 5-46; the 58 original cards follow at 47-104. Each scored band independently uses descending unrounded CoD/duration, then higher CoD, shorter duration and ascending stable backlog ID. Integer cross-multiplication verifies order. This is not one globally ratio-sorted list across the band boundary.
- **Interpretation:** ranks 1-104 are unique. The original baseline positions shift by 46, preserving estimates and relative order. The extra scenario band is an explicit user priority decision, not inflated scores. The final ID tie-break is administrative; overlapping benefits are not added as an epic value estimate.
- **Re-estimation:** replace these hypotheses with validated product/customer inputs and engineering duration estimates after the pilot, discovery, or a material scope change; recompute all affected ranks. Future discovery estimates do not cover future implementation.

<a id="kano"></a>

## Kano definitions

- `dissatisfier`: must-be/basic expectation. Absence causes dissatisfaction; the term does **not** mean the feature itself is undesirable.
- `satisfier`: performance or utility expectation. Better execution is expected to increase satisfaction.
- `delighter`: potentially valuable extra beyond baseline expectations; absence may be acceptable.

Every item has exactly one primary tag and an item-specific rationale. These are analyst hypotheses, not validated Kano survey responses. Validate functional/dysfunctional customer questions and do not infer a category solely from mention frequency.
