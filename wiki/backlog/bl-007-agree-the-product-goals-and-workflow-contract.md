## User story

As a product sponsor, I want a shared process and scope contract so that the team solves the intended customer problem without expanding the POC by accident.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The process covers scoped collection, report, owner decision, support or workshop, and a no-change outcome.
2. The contract names intended application-team users, responsible functions, and explicit R1 versus future boundaries.
3. Broad organizational training, global scheduled execution, landing-zone redesign, and per-policy exception implementation are recorded outside R1.

## Priority and Kano rationale

- **Cost of delay:** Misaligned goals can create rework across every subsequent product and workshop slice.
- **Duration estimate:** A small agreed process outline and scope contract, not implementation of the entire journey.
- **Kano hypothesis:** A shared understanding of purpose and boundaries is a baseline prerequisite for a credible engagement.
- **Reassessment:** Confirm with product, customer, engineering, and relevant control owners after the first evidence review. No score is a customer-stated priority, measured saving, or delivery promise.
