## User story

As a product lead, I want a representative non-FinOps Zava team to review the workflow so that the initial release is not tailored only to its creators.

## Acceptance criteria

1. An authorized representative application team reviews its report, prior actions, relevant support, and proposed next steps.
2. Feedback records actionable, missing, confusing, and out-of-scope content against stable finding and backlog IDs.
3. The pilot review explicitly accepts or rejects R1 readiness; if no team participates, validation remains incomplete.

## Priority and Kano rationale

- **Cost of delay:** A FinOps-only walkthrough could optimize the wrong user journey and undermine broader adoption.
- **Duration estimate:** A bounded participant-approved pilot and feedback review; recruitment and availability are unverified.
- **Kano hypothesis:** Evidence of fit to the intended audience should improve the relevance of the product and workshops.
- **Reassessment:** Confirm with product, customer, engineering, and relevant control owners after the first evidence review. No score is a customer-stated priority, measured saving, or delivery promise.
