## User story

As a working-session organizer, I want a short preparation checklist so that the next longer session has concrete goals and usable inputs.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The checklist covers intended decisions, source reports, required participants, open questions, and desired outputs.
2. The proposed preparation fits the requested half-hour discussion and identifies who must confirm the longer session's objectives.
3. The artifact distinguishes preparation requested, preparation held, and longer session scheduled without claiming any meeting has occurred.

## Priority and Kano rationale

- **Cost of delay:** Unfocused long sessions delay concrete decisions, though this preparation does not itself deliver product functionality.
- **Duration estimate:** A small reusable checklist and agenda artifact reflecting the requested preparation timebox.
- **Kano hypothesis:** More focused preparation should improve the usefulness of longer collaborative sessions.
- **Reassessment:** Confirm with product, customer, engineering, and relevant control owners after the first evidence review. No score is a customer-stated priority, measured saving, or delivery promise.
