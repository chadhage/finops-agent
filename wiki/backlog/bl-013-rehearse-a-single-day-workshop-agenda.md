## User story

As a workshop sponsor, I want a timed agenda that fits a single day so that teams can receive focused help without repeating discovery.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The sponsor defines the working-day timebox, breaks, essential topics, and explicit overflow topics before rehearsal.
2. A timed rehearsal uses the handoff and intake artifacts and records whether the agreed timebox is met.
3. If content does not fit, the decision record narrows the agenda or revisits packaging rather than asserting success.

## Priority and Kano rationale

- **Cost of delay:** An oversized workshop can block adoption of an otherwise useful report-to-help process.
- **Duration estimate:** A bounded agenda design and timed rehearsal, with participant availability still unverified.
- **Kano hypothesis:** An agenda that fits the customer's time and problem scope should improve workshop utility.
- **Reassessment:** Confirm with product, customer, engineering, and relevant control owners after the first evidence review. No score is a customer-stated priority, measured saving, or delivery promise.
