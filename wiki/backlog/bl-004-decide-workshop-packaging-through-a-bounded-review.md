## User story

As a workshop sponsor, I want to compare one combined offering with separate sessions so that the format fits the findings and available time.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The comparison covers FinOps and security topics, target participants, required preparation, and trade-offs for both formats.
2. The customer decision or remaining unanswered question is recorded with its rationale.
3. The output distinguishes a proposed format from a booked or delivered workshop.

## Priority and Kano rationale

- **Cost of delay:** Unresolved packaging makes invitations and agenda design less reliable but does not block all report work.
- **Duration estimate:** A short option comparison and customer decision record, not delivery of multiple full workshops.
- **Kano hypothesis:** A format that fits the required topics should make the workshop more useful and accessible.
- **Reassessment:** Confirm with product, customer, engineering, and relevant control owners after the first evidence review. No score is a customer-stated priority, measured saving, or delivery promise.
