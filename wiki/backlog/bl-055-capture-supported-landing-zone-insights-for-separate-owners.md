## User story

As a platform stakeholder, I want relevant high-benefit observations handed to the separate landing-zone activity so that useful evidence is retained without expanding the POC.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Each parked observation records supporting evidence, a proposed benefit hypothesis, uncertainty, and the separate platform owner.
2. Unsupported ideas are not promoted to confirmed findings or implementation commitments.
3. The disposition explicitly retains, refers, or rejects the observation and leaves current product scope unchanged.

## Priority and Kano rationale

- **Cost of delay:** The customer explicitly parks this topic and cautions against rabbit holes in the current activity.
- **Duration estimate:** A small evidence-and-benefit handoff record, not landing-zone design or implementation.
- **Kano hypothesis:** A well-supported adjacent insight may create additional future value, but it is not required for the local product to work.
- **Reassessment:** Confirm with product, customer, engineering, and relevant control owners after the first evidence review. No score is a customer-stated priority, measured saving, or delivery promise.
